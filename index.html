    Specifications

    Built to last. Finished to impress.

    Every material is chosen for durability, safety and everyday comfort.

    Download the brochure
    1. 01

      Structure

      RCC frame and pile foundation to BIS standards, with 9 in external and 4.5 in internal clay-brick walls.

    2. 02

      Floors & ceilings

      120 x 60 cm double-charged vitrified tiles in bedrooms, living and dining. Gypsum ceilings in living, dining and bedrooms.

    3. 03

      Doors

      Teak main doors and frames; factory-finished flush internal doors with teak frames; WPC washroom doors with quality hardware.

    4. 04

      Windows

      2.5-track UPVC windows with mosquito mesh. Enamel-finished MS security grilles with 10 mm square rods.

    5. 05

      Paint finishes

      Two coats of Asian interior emulsion over putty and primer. Asian weatherproof emulsion on exterior surfaces.

    6. 06

      Electrical

      Salzer concealed wiring for 3-phase supply, with PVC-insulated copper cables. Lighting and appliance points; modular switches with ELCB and MCB protection.

    7. 07

      Bathrooms

      Waterproofed walls up to 1 ft, anti-skid floor tiles, glazed wall tiles, UPVC ventilators, and quality Cera/Roca or equivalent fittings, including an EWC with a flush tank.

    8. 08

      Kitchen & storage

      Granite platform, stainless-steel sink and a 2 ft tile dado. Appliance power points and cement plank shelving in bedrooms, dining and kitchen.
